# Code of Conduct

OuterSpace OS welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Treat other participants with respect, give constructive feedback and focus
technical disagreements on evidence and behavior.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks and
publishing private information without consent are unacceptable. These standards
apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and spaces where someone is
representing the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@outerspace.sh](mailto:support@outerspace.sh).
Maintainers will review reports with care, protect the reporter's privacy and
respond proportionately. Responses can include a warning, removal of content or
a temporary or permanent participation restriction. Maintainers are accountable
to the same standards. Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).
